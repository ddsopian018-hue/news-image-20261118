A newspaper front page that sums up Wednesday 18 November 2026 in one image: Trump meets Marcos in Manila after the Quad's first summit in two years, markets brace for Nvidia's results, Cyclone Akvan bears down on the Bay of Bengal, Taiwan seeks $14bn in US arms, Amazon bets $7bn on green energy in India, and Zelensky reports new Kyiv talks — with briefs on UEFA backing Diallo against Infantino, Pope Leo struck by a rosary in Peru, and Baidu's chief warning of an AI bubble. The image is drawn in-session by `draw.py` (PIL, no stock photos) and `main.ts` copies it to the output directory.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

To redraw the image from scratch:

```sh
python3 draw.py
```
